# Trademarks

Steward-GRC™ and the Steward-GRC logo are unregistered (common-law) trademarks of
the Steward-GRC project. They're not registered yet, which is why you'll see ™ rather than ®. Applications
to register them are being prepared, so full registered protection is pending;
common-law rights already apply from first use.

## What's fine, no permission needed

- Referring to the project, or any of its services, by name in your own writing,
  talks, docs or code.
- Saying your product or integration "works with Steward-GRC" or "is based on
  Steward-GRC".
- Redistributing the project unmodified, under its own name.

## What needs permission

- Running Steward-GRC as a hosted or SaaS offering under the Steward-GRC name.
- Using "Steward-GRC" (or anything confusingly close to it) as the name of your own
  product or company.
- Distributing a modified fork under the Steward-GRC name. A fork needs a name of
  its own.

## Logo

The Steward-GRC logo may be used unmodified, and only to refer to the project
itself. It may not be altered, combined with another mark, or used in a way that
implies endorsement.

## Questions

Not sure whether a use is covered? Open an issue on
